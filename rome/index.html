    Method and limits

    Drawings carry numbered keynotes (listed in each title block) and the renders carry labelled callouts. In the 3D viewer, tap a pin or a name in the Annotations list for a short note.

    The drawings and the 3D models share one geometry, built in metres from the published figures listed alongside. Where no published figure was found (the Pantheon's stepped drum rings, the heights of Sant'Agnese and the frontage buildings, the surrounding city blocks), values are estimated from proportion and marked ≈ on the sheets. Street context is schematic.

    Treat this as a study set for analysis and presentation. It is not a measured heritage survey.
