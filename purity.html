Matter Out of Place

Dirt, the anthropologist Mary Douglas wrote, is matter out of place. The same earth is ground in the street and a stain on a white floor.

Walk in from the street to the inner room. Whatever is on your feet comes with you. The basin has a tank of water.

Arrow keys or WASD to walk. Hold Space at the basin to wash.
